# Product pitch (100 words)

Smart Market Watch answers one question: what changed since I last looked, and does it matter? Each user's last-seen price per symbol is stored server-side, so every return visit compares against what they actually saw, not yesterday's close. Moves are scored against each stock's own volatility and volume, so 2% in a quiet name outranks 2% in a volatile one, and every alert explains why with evidence. It covers NSE, BSE, US, LSE and other exchanges, each with its own currency and session hours. Quotes are labelled LIVE, DELAYED, STALE or UNAVAILABLE, and a provider outage never overwrites good data.
